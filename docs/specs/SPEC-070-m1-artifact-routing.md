# SPEC-070 - M1 artifact-family routing

- Version: 0.1
- Maturity: BETA
- Evidence: Sessions 001-060 and 062
- Related questions: RQ-225-RQ-229

## Contract

Every non-directory ISO member must map to exactly one bounded family. Positive
ELF, U-Boot, Intel HEX, METAINFO and YIM claims require their documented
structural gates. Extension-only LOD and opaque BIN/SW labels remain explicitly
non-semantic.

Every family must name prior evidence, a next milestone and a deeper-analysis
route. `COMPLETE` means coverage and routing are complete, not that all formats
are decoded.
